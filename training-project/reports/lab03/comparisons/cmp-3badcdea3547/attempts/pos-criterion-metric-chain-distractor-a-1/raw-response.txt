  "proposed_text": "In evaluation design, a criterion specifies the quality property or standard that a system must satisfy, acting as the target benchmark. A metric is then defined as a measurable value that can be compared against the criterion to determine whether that property holds, providing the quantitative basis for judging system compliance. This relationship is inherently evaluative: the criterion establishes the requirement, and the metric supplies the measurement needed to assess its fulfillment, enabling objective quality assessment."
